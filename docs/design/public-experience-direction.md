# Aelora public experience direction

## Objective

Make the landing and authentication experience feel authored, credible, and directly connected to the working Aelora product. The public pages should communicate solar monitoring and forecasting without relying on generic feature-card grids, decorative gradients, or stock dashboard imagery.

## Direction: Solar observatory

Aelora is presented as a precise energy instrument with an editorial voice:

- graphite black provides the technical, observatory-like base;
- warm ivory separates explanatory content from the live instrument surface;
- solar amber represents generation and daylight;
- electric blue represents household demand and data;
- compact mono labels provide measurement context while large display type carries the story.

The hero contains a purpose-built energy visualization rather than an unrelated decorative image. The landing page moves through a clear narrative: observe the current system, anticipate the coming week, begin with a digital twin, then enter or create a workspace.

## Inspiration boundaries

Igloo informed the restrained dark canvas, sense of depth, and sparse interface treatment. Lando Norris informed the confident typography, editorial pacing, and contrast between large statements and compact factual labels. No brand assets, layouts, wording, or distinctive interaction patterns are copied.

## Interaction and accessibility rules

- Keep the page useful without client-side JavaScript.
- Use one `h1` per page and landmarks with descriptive labels.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus and a skip link.
- Give the chart an accessible name and description.
- Keep primary hit targets at least 44 pixels high.
- Use explicit color and transform transitions only.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep the mobile experience linear and free of horizontal overflow.

## Authentication continuity

The sign-in page uses the same palette and energy language as the landing page. Its supporting panel shows recognizable live-site context, while the form remains quiet, direct, and high contrast. Existing validation, secure callback handling, routes, and authentication behavior remain unchanged.
